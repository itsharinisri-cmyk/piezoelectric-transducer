Piezoelectric Transducer

Explore how mechanical pressure and vibration generate electrical charge.

Interactive 3D Model

Rotate the model, or tap View in AR on a compatible phone.

Working Principle

1. Mechanical input: Force or vibration deforms the piezoelectric material.

2. Charge generation: The material develops electrical charge.

3. Electrical output: Electrodes collect the charge for measurement.

Applications

Vibration sensors, impact detection, pressure measurement, ultrasonic devices and piezoelectric pickups.

Measurement Circuit

A high-input-impedance amplifier or charge amplifier can condition the sensor signal before it is measured using an oscilloscope or ADC.
