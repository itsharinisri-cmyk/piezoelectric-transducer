2. Working principle

1

Apply force

Pressure, vibration, or impact stresses a piezoelectric material such as quartz or a piezoceramic.

2

Charge develops

The material develops electrical charge on its surfaces due to the direct piezoelectric effect.

3

Measure output

A suitable high-input-impedance circuit or charge amplifier measures the signal.

Important: Piezoelectric sensors are especially useful for dynamic measurements such as vibration, impact, and changing pressure. They are generally not ideal for measuring perfectly static force over long periods because charge can leak away.

3. Simplified circuit diagram

A common measurement arrangement uses a piezoelectric element connected to a charge amplifier, followed by an output display or data-acquisition system.

4. Applications

Vibration sensing

Monitoring machinery and rotating equipment.

Impact detection

Detecting knocks, impacts, or sudden force changes.

Dynamic pressure

Measuring rapidly changing pressure.

Ultrasound

Converting electrical energy to mechanical waves and back in suitable piezoelectric devices.
